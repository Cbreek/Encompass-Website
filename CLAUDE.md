# Working with Chrissy (Greytone Digital)

Chrissy is new to AI tools. Explain simply, with analogies, and keep steps short.

## Prototype workflow (always follow this)

1. **Lovable** designs and builds the prototype ("makes the wedding dress").
2. **ChatGPT + Chrissy** handle art direction, creative strategy and business development.
3. **Claude Code** gets the prototype last and steers from there: final review,
   final tailoring and finishing, hosting the private prototype on **Netlify**
   (temporary viewing link, like ferrigno-vineyards-prototype.netlify.app), and the pitch email.
4. Chrissy does **not** go back to Lovable once Claude Code has it. Never send her back there.

Chrissy works in Safari tabs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Lovable), not the Claude desktop app.
She must be able to SEE and approve every final change: give her a link she can open in
Safari (the Netlify prototype link), not a zip.

## Before sharing any screenshot or preview

Lovable projects keep some images in Lovable's own storage (`*.asset.json` files whose
`url` starts with `/__l5e/assets-v1/`). Those images do not load outside Lovable. Copy the
real image files into the project before building, hosting or screenshotting, and never
send a screenshot with missing images.

## Pitch emails

Follow the Ferrigno Vineyards pitch structure: personal hook, what the prototype is,
homepage screenshot, temporary private link, "this is a concept, examples are placeholders",
the bigger idea, feature list, Google visibility, price with friend discount
(or a lower price for two introductions), what it covers, a warm close, then the signature
"Chrissy Breek, Founder | Greytone Digital".

Past pitches: Ferrigno Vineyards (sent), Encompass (sent), Mike Grover (not yet sent),
Shelene Bryan (in progress, Nov 2026).
