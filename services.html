Integrated Technology Solutions

From infrastructure backbone to user-facing systems—every solution we design, install, and commission is engineered to perform reliably for the long term.

Our Capabilities

A Full Spectrum of Low-Voltage Technology

Encompass Technology Partners is a single-source Design + Build firm. We handle the full technology scope—so your project has one accountable partner from design through commissioning.

01

Audio / Visual Integration

Professional AV systems for corporate boardrooms, executive conference rooms, training rooms, lobbies, and large-format display environments. We design with end-user simplicity in mind—intuitive, reliable systems that anyone can operate.

  • Display technology: LED, LCD, video wall, projection
  • Distributed audio and architectural speaker systems
  • Digital signage and wayfinding systems
  • Premium residential media rooms and home theater

02

Conferencing Systems

Full-featured collaboration environments built around modern unified communications platforms. We integrate hardware, software, and room controls into a seamless experience for in-person and remote participants alike.

  • Video conferencing hardware (camera, codec, mic, display)
  • Platform integration: Teams, Zoom, Webex, Google Meet
  • Room scheduling displays and occupancy management
  • Acoustic treatment and ceiling microphone arrays
Discuss Your Conference Room
03

Control Systems

Centralized control platforms that put the entire room or building's technology at your fingertips. From single-room touchpanels to enterprise-wide management platforms, we program intuitive, purpose-built control experiences.

  • Touchpanel programming and user interface design
  • Lighting control and shade integration
  • HVAC, AV, and security system unification
  • Remote monitoring and facility management platforms
Discuss Control Systems
04

Ethernet & Structured Cabling

The physical foundation every technology system depends on. We design and install enterprise-grade copper and fiber optic backbone infrastructure that meets or exceeds ANSI/TIA standards—properly labeled, documented, and built to last.

  • CAT6 / CAT6A copper horizontal and backbone cabling
  • Single-mode and multimode fiber optic installations
  • MDF / IDF room design, rack build-out, and patching
  • Full testing, certification, and as-built documentation
Discuss Cabling Infrastructure
05

WiFi & Wireless Infrastructure

High-performance enterprise wireless design from the ground up. With Sean Smith's background in large-scale RF engineering and DAS deployment, ETP delivers wireless solutions capable of handling the most demanding commercial and venue environments.

  • Enterprise WiFi 6/6E design and deployment
  • Distributed Antenna Systems (DAS) for indoor cellular
  • RF site surveys and coverage modeling
  • Private LTE / 5G network planning and deployment
Discuss Wireless Design
06

Physical Security Systems

Comprehensive electronic security solutions for commercial buildings, corporate campuses, and multi-tenant properties. We design, install, and commission integrated security systems that protect people, assets, and information.

  • IP CCTV surveillance: cameras, NVRs, and video management
  • Card access control and credential management
  • Intrusion detection and alarm systems
  • Visitor management and intercom integration
Discuss Security Systems
07

Speech Privacy & Sound Masking

Electronic sound masking systems that raise the ambient noise floor to reduce the intelligibility of conversations—improving acoustic privacy in open offices, healthcare facilities, financial environments, and legal spaces without impacting productivity.

  • Open-plan office sound masking design and tuning
  • HIPAA-compliant healthcare speech privacy solutions
  • Paging system integration and background music
  • Acoustic consulting and zone-level system tuning
Discuss Speech Privacy
08

Network Infrastructure

Complete network infrastructure solutions from the physical layer through switching and routing. We partner with building owners, IT departments, and GCs to design infrastructure that supports today's demands and tomorrow's growth.

  • Network switching, routing, and VLAN configuration
  • PoE infrastructure for IP cameras, phones, and APs
  • UPS, power conditioning, and surge protection
  • Infrastructure documentation and ongoing maintenance
