Our Story

Founded on Experience. Built for Commercial Technology.

Encompass Technology Partners was established in 2019 by three seasoned professionals who spent their careers at some of Southern California's most respected technology integration and telecommunications firms.

Before founding ETP, our partners worked alongside each other at On Target Solutions, where they developed deep expertise in large-scale low-voltage infrastructure, fiber-optic deployment, and enterprise network systems.

Each brought a distinct specialty—wireless engineering and DAS, structured cabling and physical security, and commercial AV and account management—creating a firm capable of handling the complete technology scope of any commercial build.

  • Rooted in the SoCal construction and real estate market
  • Background spans utility infrastructure, telecom, AV, and enterprise wireless
  • Active on Procore for large-scale West Coast commercial coordination

How We Work

Our Approach to Every Project

01
