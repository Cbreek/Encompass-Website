What We Do

Integrated Technology Solutions, End to End

From boardroom displays to enterprise wireless networks, we design and build the technology infrastructure that powers how modern organizations work.

Audio / Visual Integration

Custom AV design and installation for boardrooms, huddle spaces, lobbies, and large-format displays. Every system built for clarity and ease of use.

Conferencing Systems

Unified communications platforms, video conferencing infrastructure, and collaboration-room build-outs designed for modern distributed teams.

Control Systems

Centralized room control, lighting automation, and integrated building technology management that simplifies complex environments.

Ethernet & Structured Cabling

Enterprise-grade copper and fiber optic backbone infrastructure, designed and installed to industry standards for long-term reliability.

Wi-Fi & Wireless Networks

High-density enterprise Wi-Fi design, DAS (Distributed Antenna Systems), and cellular infrastructure for campuses, facilities, and commercial spaces.

Physical Security

CCTV surveillance, IP camera systems, access control, and intrusion detection engineered for commercial buildings and enterprise campuses.

Speech Privacy

Sound masking and speech privacy systems for open-plan offices, healthcare facilities, and sensitive commercial environments where confidentiality matters.

Network Infrastructure

Full low-voltage infrastructure planning, from MDF/IDF room design to rack build-outs and patch management—complete, clean, and documented.

About the Firm

Three Partners. One Standard of Excellence.

Encompass Technology Partners was founded in 2019 by Sean Sutton, Jason Voss, and Sean Smith—three veterans of Southern California's most respected technology integration and telecommunications firms.

Together, they bring expertise in large-scale wireless engineering, enterprise AV, structured cabling, physical security, and low-voltage systems programming—delivering complete technology infrastructure for commercial clients across the West Coast.
